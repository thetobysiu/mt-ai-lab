- Combat: You hit first. Your dmg = ATK - Enemy DEF. Enemy dmg = Enemy ATK - Your DEF. If your DEF >= Enemy ATK, you take 0 damage. If your ATK <= Enemy DEF, you deal 0 damage.

- Goal: Kill the Boss alive with MAXIMUM remaining HP. Survival is a constraint; HP maximization is the objective.

STRATEGY:

2. Redefining "Optional" (CRITICAL): Engine labels like `[OPTIONAL]` mean "not required to open the Boss door," NOT "skip to save HP."

   - NEVER skip a fight with negative `effCost` just because it is labeled optional or not on the direct path.
3. Termination Condition: Only fight the Boss when ALL accessible actions with negative `effCost` are completed.
   - Before fighting Boss, verify `unlock tree` and `lookahead`. If any "optional" fight/gem reduces Boss damage by MORE than its cost, take it first.
   - Do not stop at "survivable". Stop only when no remaining action yields a net HP gain.
4. Stat Efficiency & Ordering:
   - Prioritize actions with the most negative `effCost`.
   - Delay fights that block stat collection. If Fight A unlocks Gem B which reduces damage for Fight A, ensure you have Gem B before Fight A if possible (check `ORDER` annotations).
   - Respect `ORDER` annotations: If an annotation says taking Fight A before Fight B saves HP, follow that sequence strictly.
5. Path vs. Gain: Do not prioritize unlocking doors/keys over negative `effCost` actions unless the door is strictly required to access the negative `effCost` actions.
   - Calculate: `Net Gain = (Boss Dmg Reduction + Unlocked HP) - (Path Cost)`. If `Net Gain > 0`, take the path.

OUTPUT:
Provide short reasoning based on the annotations (effCost, lookahead, ORDER, unlock tree), then end with: FINAL_CHOICE: <number>
