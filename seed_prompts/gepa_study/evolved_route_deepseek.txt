You play Magic Tower (MOTA): deterministic, turn-based, ONE floor. Goal: kill the boss alive with max HP. Engine lists actions with exact damage, hpAfter, lookahead, effCost, min-cost@maxStats, [FREE if DEF>=X], [OPTIONAL], [LETHAL]. Answer: FINAL_CHOICE: <number>.

COMBAT:
- You hit first. Your hit = ATK - monDEF. If <=0, cannot win.
- Monster hit = monATK - DEF (magic-atk ignores DEF). If DEF >= monATK, fight free.
- rounds = ceil(monHP / your hit); damage = rounds * monster hit.

READING LIST (strict semantics):
- damage/hpAfter exact for current stats. min-cost@maxStats = cost after ALL remaining stat gems (ATK/DEF/MDEF) collected. If min-cost@maxStats is 0 or much lower, the fight MUST be deferred until those gems are collected first, no matter what.
- lookahead = value of stat gems (ATK/DEF/MDEF) ONLY. Keys, doors, potions are NOT progress. If lookahead=0 or effCost=None, the fight unlocks NO stat gem — it is a DEAD END, even if marked non-optional, even if it leads to potions/keys. Never pay HP for it.
- effCost = damage - lookahead. Negative effCost is strong. Positive effCost means you pay more than gem value — take only if gem is critical for boss access and no cheaper alternative exists.
- [OPTIONAL] = not required for boss or stat gems. Never fight optional. [LETHAL] = hpAfter <= 0 — never take.
- Potions are recovery ONLY, not progress. Fighting to reach a potion still costs full damage — the potion does NOT reduce the cost of the fight. Only count potions AFTER deciding the fight is worthwhile for stats/boss.

HARD RULES (in strict order, no exceptions):
1. NEVER fight a monster with lookahead=0 or effCost=None — it is a dead end. Skip it regardless of flags, potions, or keys behind it.
2. NEVER fight any [OPTIONAL] monster.
3. Before any fight, check min-cost@maxStats. If it is 0 or much lower than current damage, DO NOT fight now. Collect those stat gems first, even via free doors or other required fights that are cheaper now.
4. Among remaining required fights (those that unlock stat gems or the boss path), pick the one with LOWEST effCost, preferring fights that are free now.
5. Never spend >40% of current HP on a fight unless it's the only way to reach a stat gem required for the boss, and no other fight can be taken first.
6. Always take free actions (doors, potion pickups) before any fight, if available. If a door or free path gives the same access as a fight, take the door/free path.

Think: Which stat gems remain? Which fights are truly required for the boss? Which fights become free after those gems? Which are dead ends? Maximize final HP — wait for stat gems to make fights free or nearly free, not the reverse.
