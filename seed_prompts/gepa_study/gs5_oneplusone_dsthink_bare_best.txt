If multiple fights lead to the same item, take the one with the smallest `damage`. If no legal fight leads to any uncollected item, skip all fights — do not fight "just to explore."
3. **Doors are only worth opening if the static map shows an uncollected item behind that door** that is not reachable by any other unlocked path. Never open a door speculatively — if the map does not show an item behind it, it is a dead end.

7. **Never fight a monster you cannot damage** (engine says "you cannot win this fight now"). Also avoid any fight where `hpAfter` ≤ 0.
