by sequencing fights so that DEF/ATK upgrades are collected BEFORE expensive fights.

  When a fight shows a large "save=Z HP" annotation, collect all accessible DEF/ATK gems first,
  THEN fight. Every point of DEF/ATK gained reduces the cost of ALL subsequent fights.

  do it even if the lookahead effCost is near zero — the gem's value compounds across future fights.
  Example: #203@(9,5) costs 55 HP but unlocks a DEF gem (+1 DEF) and a Red Potion.
  That DEF gem saves HP on every subsequent fight. Do it early.
